SMASH T.V. NATIVE
=================

Start the game:
  Windows: double-click smashtv.exe
  macOS:   open "Smash TV.app" (first time: right-click > Open)
  Linux:   ./smashtv   (run ./install.sh once to add it to your app menu)

FIRST RUN: the game needs your Smash T.V. arcade ROMs (MAME "smashtv" set).
Drag smashtv.zip, or the folder holding the 14 ROM files, onto the window,
or press Enter to pick the zip. The files are checked and copied once, so
the original can be moved afterwards. Tip: put smashtv.zip next to the
program and it is picked up automatically.

CONTROLS
  Gamepad:  left stick / d-pad = move, right stick = fire (A/B/X/Y also fire)
            Start = start/continue, Back = coin, Guide or Back+Start = menu
  Keyboard: WASD = move, arrow keys or IJKL = fire, 1/Enter = start, 5 = coin
            Esc = menu, F5/F9 = save/load state, F11 or Alt+Enter = fullscreen
  A second gamepad plays as player 2.

"Unlimited continues" (on by default, change in the Esc menu) drops a coin
for you whenever you press Start.

Your settings, high scores and save state live in:
  Windows: %APPDATA%\SmashTVNative\smashtv\
  macOS:   ~/Library/Application Support/SmashTVNative/smashtv/
  Linux:   ~/.local/share/SmashTVNative/smashtv/
A log.txt in that folder helps if something goes wrong.
